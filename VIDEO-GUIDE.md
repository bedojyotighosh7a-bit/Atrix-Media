# First three portfolio videos

Use these as practical briefs, not claims that a client project has already happened. Use footage you own or have permission to show. Match on-screen claims to the actual business.

## 1. From setup to celebration

**Quote:** “Before the guests arrive, the details speak.”

**Suggested video:** 60–90 seconds; create a separate 20–30-second vertical version.

- Open on the finished setup for two seconds.
- Show the empty venue and explain the brief in one sentence.
- Capture three details: flowers, lighting and table/stage finish.
- Include a short setup moment with the team’s permission.
- Return to the wide reveal.
- End with the service area and an invitation to share event date, venue and guest count.

**Extra posts:** a three-slide before/after carousel; a reel on one design choice; stories answering how far ahead a client should enquire.

## 2. The thinking behind the room

**Quote:** “A beautiful room. Decisions that make it work.”

**Suggested video:** 2–3 minutes; cut three 20–40-second reels.

- Hook: explain the homeowner’s practical problem.
- Show the layout and one storage decision.
- Explain a material choice and a lighting decision.
- Use before footage only when it actually shows that project.
- Close with the kind of projects the business accepts and its enquiry step.

**Extra posts:** a room-detail carousel; an FAQ about project process; a reel explaining one decision. Do not claim project costs or timelines without owner approval.

## 3. A closer look at the material

**Quote:** “The right material starts with the right questions.”

**Suggested video:** 60–120 seconds; create two short vertical clips.

- Open with a close-up of texture and finish.
- Explain intended applications using confirmed supplier information.
- Show sample options and the questions a buyer should ask.
- Include composition, care, availability or minimum-order information only when verified.
- End with a sample-enquiry CTA asking for application and quantity.

**Extra posts:** a buyer checklist carousel; texture close-up reel; common sample-order questions in stories.

## Before publishing

1. Review permissions and the accuracy of every product/service claim.
2. Upload to YouTube, add a descriptive title and thumbnail, and enable embedding.
3. Paste the URL into its slot in videos.js.
4. Update the visible card title/description in about.html if your subject changed.
5. Test playback on phone and desktop after upload. Some videos cannot embed due to their permissions or restrictions.
