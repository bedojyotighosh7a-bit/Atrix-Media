Privacy notice

Updated 18 November 2026

What you share

Our enquiry form asks for your business and contact details, location, social profile or website, selected service, goals and optional budget. Please do not send passwords, payment-card details or sensitive personal information.

Why we use it

We use your information to review your business, prepare an audit or proposal, reply to your enquiry and keep a record of our conversation. Form consent covers contact about your enquiry, not unrelated marketing subscriptions.

How enquiries are handled

The form sends your enquiry through Google Apps Script to our business inbox. Google and our website hosting provider may process technical information needed to operate these services. Our site loads fonts from Google Fonts; your browser contacts Google when loading them. Links to Instagram are governed by that platform’s own policies. When you choose to play a published portfolio video, the site loads a YouTube privacy-enhanced embed. YouTube may then process playback and technical information under its own policies. Empty video spaces do not contact YouTube.

Access and retention

Information is used by the Atryx team and service providers needed to handle your enquiry. We aim to remove inactive enquiries after six months unless an ongoing client relationship or required business record justifies keeping them longer. We do not sell enquiry details.

Your choices

You can ask to correct or delete your enquiry information, or stop further contact, by replying to our email or messaging our Instagram account. We may need to confirm that a request relates to your own information.

Browser storage

This website does not add advertising pixels or analytics cookies. The form temporarily keeps a submission identifier in memory to help prevent duplicate requests during retries. It does not save your form answers in browser storage.
