Working together

Before we get started.

Do you only work with wedding and interior businesses?

These are useful starting points for our content approach, alongside material and product businesses. We can discuss other Bengaluru businesses when the offer, assets and scope are a good fit.

Where are shoots available?

Bengaluru only. We agree locations, hours, crew and access before booking. Multiple locations, travel time, transport and parking are quoted before you commit.

Can I supply my own content?

Yes. Essentials and Managed Presence use your images and footage. We provide a brief explaining what to capture. Extra shoots are optional and separately priced.

What happens in the free audit?

We review your public Instagram profile and share three practical improvements. No account password is needed. The audit is a short review, not a free content package.

Do you guarantee customers or sales?

No. We deliver the agreed content and management work, track enquiries where possible, and improve from the evidence. Sales also depend on your offer, pricing, audience, availability and follow-up.

How do approvals and cancellation work?

You nominate one approver and receive content in batches. One consolidated revision round is included per batch. Late feedback can move publication dates. You can stop the next monthly renewal; your agreement sets out treatment of completed work and committed expenses.
